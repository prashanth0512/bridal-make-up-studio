Overview

Flawless coverage that feels like nothing at all

Airbrush foundation is applied as a fine mist, so it blurs texture without the heaviness of thick layers. It is ideal for long summer ceremonies and outdoor venues.

Because the product never touches a sponge or brush, it is also one of the most hygienic ways to apply a base.

  • Silicone-based airbrush foundation
  • Sweat, humidity and tear resistant
  • Airbrushed blush & highlight
  • Lashes and touch-up kit
