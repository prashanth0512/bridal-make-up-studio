Overview

Hair that holds its shape from mehendi to vidaai

From a classic jasmine bun to Hollywood waves, we plan every style around your face shape, your dupatta and your jewellery so everything sits together perfectly.

We prep the hair for grip and volume first, then pin and set it to last the entire day without feeling heavy.

  • Prep, volume & heat protection
  • Fresh flowers or accessory setting
  • Dupatta and veil securing
  • Extensions on request

On The Day

Your appointment, step by step

A calm, unhurried timeline planned backwards from your ceremony time, so you are ready early and never rushed.

  1. 01
    2 hrs before

    Consult & prep

    We confirm the style, then wash or refresh and protect the hair.

  2. 02
    1 hr 45 before

    Volume & texture

    Blow-dry, curl or crimp to give the style a strong base.

  3. 03
    1 hr 15 before

    Sculpt & pin

    The style is built, shaped and secured section by section.

  4. 04
    45 mins before

    Flowers & accessories

    Fresh flowers and hair jewellery added and pinned in place.

  5. 05
    20 mins before

    Dupatta & final set

    Dupatta or veil secured and a final hold spray.
