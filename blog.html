Planning Guide

Your bridal beauty countdown

A simple timeline our brides follow so nothing is left to the last minute.

  1. 6 months before

    Book your artist

    Lock in your date, share your ceremonies and start a mood board of looks you love.

  2. 3 months before

    Start your skin plan

    Begin a gentle, consistent routine and book any facials well ahead of the day.

  3. 1 month before

    Trial & finalise

    Try your full look with your outfit, refine shades and confirm timings.

  4. 1 week before

    Rest & hydrate

    No new products or treatments. Sleep well, drink water and let your skin settle.
