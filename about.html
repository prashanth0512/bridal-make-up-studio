Who We Are

From one chair to a studio of artists

Blush & Bloom began in 2014 with a single makeup chair and one belief: every bride deserves to feel calm, confident and completely herself on her wedding day.

Today our team of fifteen artists styles brides across India for haldi, mehendi, weddings and receptions, but every booking still starts the same way, with a conversation and a full trial.

  • Personal consultation & trial for every bride
  • Premium, skin-safe and long-wear products
  • On-time, unhurried artistry on the big day

Our Journey

Milestones along the way

  1. 2014

    The first chair

    Our founder opens a small home studio and styles her first twelve brides.

  2. 2017

    First destination wedding

    A three-day palace wedding in Udaipur, and our travel team is born.

  3. 2020

    HD & airbrush studio

    We introduce airbrush artistry and train a dedicated team of specialists.

  4. 2023

    1,000 happy brides

    A milestone we celebrated with every bride who trusted us along the way.

  5. 2026

    Our new flagship studio

    A calm, light-filled space with private bridal suites and a trial lounge.
